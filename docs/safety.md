# Safety review

This is an unfinished review checklist, not a safety certification.

Before building or testing, document:

- Applicable supplier instructions and safety data for each material.
- Intended users, location, and foreseeable contact with people, pets, or plants.
- Printing and post-processing requirements from equipment and material suppliers.
- Stability, sharp edges, small detachable parts, and mechanical failure modes.
- Water containment, drainage, cleaning access, and any nearby electrical equipment.
- Whether the exact insert product is intended for the proposed plant and water exposure.
- Experiment stop criteria and disposal procedures based on the materials used.

Record unresolved issues and their disposition in the build log. Do not label parts production-ready until the relevant checks have been completed.
