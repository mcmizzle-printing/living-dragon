## Product-page guidance and claims

The supplier describes a printable filament containing strontium aluminate and plant-supportive additives, with gradual dissolution in water. Nutrient benefit and dissolution performance remain supplier claims, not project results.

Product-specific directions specify a 0.8 mm nozzle, Cryogrip blue plate, and drying at 68°C for at least 24 hours. The embedded printing section instead names “Stick, Spark & Glow” and lists two hours of drying. Do not adopt that section as a verified profile for this product; resolve the mismatch before setting machine parameters.

## Manual limitation

The manual's general introduction advises against Bambu push-type AMS feeding and recommends manual swaps. Verify the actual machine and feed path before applying that guidance; the project has no validated P2S or H2C preset.

## Project implications — proposed, not validated

Use removable inserts with a separate structural body so insert changes can be measured and replacements fitted. First test extrusion and dry fit, then water exposure, then seed retention and growth with a comparison group. Define measurements and acceptance criteria before each trial.

Record batch, initial mass and dimensions, drying conditions, exposure duration, water conditions, retained fit, and standardized glow photographs. Keep the glow measurement separate from plant response.
